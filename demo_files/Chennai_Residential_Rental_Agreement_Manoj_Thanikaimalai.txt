LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is made and executed at Chennai on this 01st DAY OF NOVEMBER 2023.

BY AND BETWEEN:

Mrs. T.Shri Manoj, S/O Late T. Shri Manoj, residing at 3rd Main Road, Rajakilpakkam, CHENNAI (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR", which expression shall unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof mean and include their heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns) of the FIRST PART;

AND

Mr. Thanikaimalai, S/O Mr. Thanikaimalai, residing at 3rd Main Road, Rajakilpakkam, CHENNAI (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE", which expression shall unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof mean and include their heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS the LESSOR is the absolute owner of the residential property situated at 3rd Main Road, Rajakilpakkam, CHENNAI (hereinafter referred to as the "Leased Premises").

WHEREAS the LESSEE has approached the LESSOR to take the Leased Premises on lease for residential purposes, and the LESSOR has agreed to lease out the same.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH AS UNDER:

1. DURATION:
The lease is for 11 months starting from 01st Day of November 2023 to 30th September 2024. Either party can terminate this agreement by giving 30 days written notice to the other.

2. RENT AND DEPOSIT:
The monthly rent shall be INR 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand Only), payable on or before the 5th of every English calendar month. A security deposit of INR 75,000 (Rupees Seventy Five Thousand Only) shall be paid by the LESSEE to the LESSOR, which shall be interest-free and refundable at the time of vacating the premises.

3. RESTRICTIONS AND PENALTIES:
The LESSEE shall not sublet the premises or make any structural alterations. If the rent is delayed by more than 10 days, a penalty of INR 500 per day shall apply. Under Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, arbitrary penalties are void unless the claiming party can prove actual financial loss.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this Lease Agreement on the day, month, and year first above written.


Lessor: Mrs. T.Shri Manoj
Lessee: Mr. Thanikaimalai
